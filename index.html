SECE Procurement Policy

All departmental purchases above ₹25,000 require multi-tier vendor comparison, HOD approval, Central Purchase clearance, and final Management Sanction sign-off.

Approval Workflow

  1. 1. Faculty / Lab Tech / Student raises request
  2. 2. HOD reviews & endorses
  3. 3. Purchase Desk compares vendor quotes
  4. 4. Management grants final sanction
  5. 5. Purchase issues PO to vendor
  6. 6. Department confirms delivery
